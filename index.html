About us

We mainly specialise in regular and deep cleaning, and we always try to provide a friendly and reliable service. Close attention to detail is at the heart of everything we do, whether it's a weekly clean or a one-off deep clean.

Frequently asked questions

What's included in the price?

Cleaning products are included and provided by us. Cleaning equipment can be provided for an additional charge.

How long will the clean take?

Cleaning time depends on the size and condition of the property. If it needs additional time, we'll discuss this with you where possible before continuing.

Can you clean extras like ovens or fridges?

Yes — we can clean additional items such as ovens, inside windows, fridges, dishwashers and washing machines. These are charged separately.

How do I book?

You can book through WhatsApp or by phone, and hopefully through this website in future too.

Can you collect and return keys?

Yes, we can collect and return keys if required. For end-of-tenancy cleaning, if keys need to be collected from an agency, there's a small additional fee for key collection and return.

What if I need to cancel or change my booking?

We just ask for at least 24 hours' notice if you need to cancel or change your booking.

Terms & conditions

The full details of how we work together.

1 Services

Quality Gleam Services provides regular cleaning, deep cleaning, end-of-tenancy cleaning, Airbnb turnovers and post-build/construction cleaning.

We aim to provide a friendly, reliable and thorough service while paying close attention to detail.

2 Pricing and quotations

Our advertised prices are starting prices and are provided as a guide.

The final price may vary depending on factors including: size of the property, condition of the property, level of cleaning required, customer requirements, and additional services requested.

Additional services, including oven, fridge, dishwasher, washing machine and inside-window cleaning, may incur additional charges.

Post-build/construction cleaning is priced individually according to the requirements of the job.

Where additional cleaning time is required due to the size or condition of a property, additional charges may apply. Where reasonably possible, we will discuss this with the customer before carrying out additional work.

3 Bookings

Bookings can be made by phone or WhatsApp, and bookings made through the website may also be available where this facility is offered.

A booking is considered confirmed once the details of the service have been agreed with the customer.

4 Cancellation and changes

Customers must provide at least 24 hours' notice if they need to cancel or change a booking.

5 Access and keys

Customers are responsible for providing suitable access to the property at the agreed time.

Where agreed in advance, Quality Gleam Services may collect and return keys.

For end-of-tenancy cleaning, an additional fee may apply where keys need to be collected from and returned to a letting agency.

6 Cleaning equipment and products

Standard cleaning products are provided by Quality Gleam Services.

Cleaning equipment may be provided for an additional charge where agreed with the customer.

7 Property condition and additional cleaning

The quoted or estimated cleaning time is based on the information available when the booking is made.

If the condition or size of the property requires substantially more time than anticipated, additional cleaning time may be charged.

Where reasonably possible, the customer will be informed before additional work continues.

8 Customer responsibilities

Customers should inform Quality Gleam Services before the appointment of any relevant circumstances that may affect the cleaning, including particularly delicate surfaces, valuable items, specific cleaning requirements or areas requiring special care.

Customers should ensure that the property is reasonably accessible and safe for the cleaning team.

9 Additional services

Additional services requested by the customer that are not included in the original booking may incur an additional charge.

Any additional charges will be discussed with the customer where reasonably possible before the work is carried out.

10 Damage and liability

Quality Gleam Services will take reasonable care when carrying out cleaning services.

Customers should notify us as soon as reasonably possible if they believe any damage has occurred during a cleaning service so that the matter can be investigated.

We cannot accept responsibility for pre-existing damage, normal wear and tear, or damage resulting from defects, poor condition or unsuitable materials/surfaces.

11 Complaints

If you are unhappy with any aspect of our cleaning service, please contact us as soon as possible so that we can discuss the issue and, where appropriate, arrange a suitable resolution.
